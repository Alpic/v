# Ground News rating methodology and rater data (Meeting 1, topic R-1)

Scope: how Ground News rates outlets and stories, the three outside raters, free data access, and Finnish outlet coverage.
Status key: [VERIFIED] = read on the cited primary page. [SECONDARY] = from a search summary or third-party page. [UNVERIFIED] = not confirmed; check before relying on it.

## 1. Summary (for the audit)

- Ground News does not rate outlets itself. Its bias rating is the average of three outside raters: AllSides, Ad Fontes Media, Media Bias Fact Check (MBFC). [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/about-bias-ratings]
- Its Factuality Score is the average of Ad Fontes and MBFC only (AllSides is not used for factuality). [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/rating-system]
- The mapping from each rater's native scale to Ground News's 7 labels is not published. The Blindspot page gives no numeric thresholds. [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/about-bias-ratings, https://about.ground.news/blindspot]
- Ground News's own method is therefore a re-aggregation. Its quality depends on the three raters, which are themselves US-centric. [VERIFIED: https://mediabiasfactcheck.com/methodology/]
- Ground News licenses or uses rater data with mixed permission. CJR reports that AllSides says there was no formal permission; Ad Fontes says Ground News pays; MBFC says it has no agreement. [VERIFIED: https://www.cjr.org/analysis/the-business-of-balance-ground-news.php]

## 2. Ground News method, point by point

**(1) Political bias (outlet level)**
- Seven labels: Far Left, Left, Lean Left, Center, Lean Right, Right, Far Right. [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/rating-system]
- Scope: per publication, not per article. "This rating does not measure the bias of specific news articles." Analysis is "in the context of the U.S. political system." [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/about-bias-ratings]
- Inputs named: "wording, story choices and political affiliation." [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/rating-system]
- Averaging: mean of the available ratings from the three raters. If only one or two exist, Ground News uses the average of what is available. No weighting is published. [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/about-bias-ratings]
- Center is not balance: "does not necessarily represent 'balance' or 'neutrality.'" [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/rating-system]
- Outlets with no rating are excluded from the Bias Bar. [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/about-bias-ratings]

**(2) Factuality**
- Factuality Score = average of Ad Fontes and MBFC. If only one rates the outlet, that rating is used. No rating means no score. [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/about-bias-ratings]
- Five levels: Very Low, Low, Mixed, High, Very High. Stated criteria: credible sources, timeliness of corrections, added context. [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/rating-system]
- The conversion from Ad Fontes and MBFC native scales to the five levels is not published. [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/rating-system]
- Factuality is paywalled for most readers. [SECONDARY, per CJR: https://www.cjr.org/analysis/the-business-of-balance-ground-news.php]

**(3) Ownership**
- Eight categories: Media Conglomerates, Private Equity, Individual, Government, Telecom, Corporation, Independent, Other. [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/rating-system]
- Method: Ground News "researched, analyzed and hand-coded ownership data for 2,276 news outlets." Data sources are not named. [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/rating-system]
- Independent: any ownership stake above a "publicized 5% cut-off" counts as influence. [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/rating-system]
- Individual owner: net worth "in the tens of millions or greater" and wealth built outside media. [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/rating-system]
- Ownership is not stated to affect bias or factuality scores. [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/about-bias-ratings]
- Example: South China Morning Post is cited as Corporation (owned by Alibaba). [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/rating-system]

**(4) Story bias distribution and Blindspot**
- Each article is tagged with the seven labels from its publishing outlet's average rating. Articles on the same event from different outlets are merged into one story. [VERIFIED: https://about.ground.news/blindspot]
- Story distribution: the story's outlet ratings form a distribution (the page does not give the exact aggregation for the bar; [UNVERIFIED] how the bar is drawn).
- Blindspot selection: from the day's top 100 stories, compute mean and standard deviation of outlet bias ratings. Select stories with the highest mean (one side dominates) or highest standard deviation (coverage on the fringes, not the Center). [VERIFIED: https://about.ground.news/blindspot]
- Inconsistency: the overview says "the past week," but the methodology says "the day's top 100." The weekly Blindspot Report is the product. [VERIFIED: https://about.ground.news/blindspot]
- Not published: numeric values for the 7 labels, minimum sources per side, the definition of "most covered"/"most read", how many stories per report, and how daily results roll up to weekly. [VERIFIED: https://about.ground.news/blindspot]
- Earlier marketing wording: a Left Blindspot "requires fewer than 10 Left-leaning sources." [SECONDARY: https://ground.news/ search summary; Ground News's own Blindspot page does not state this threshold, so treat as [UNVERIFIED]]

**(5) Non-US and international sources**
- Not covered on the rating page beyond "analysis is done in the context of the U.S. political system." No separate method for non-US or non-English outlets is described. [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/rating-system]
- Ground News's own examples are US outlets (CNN, AP, Fox News). [VERIFIED: https://ground.news/rating-system]
- Non-US outlets appear in Ground News feeds (for example Helsingin Sanomat, interest page exists). Whether they get a rating depends on the three raters. [SECONDARY: https://ground.news/interest/helsingin-sanomat]

**(6) Known criticisms**
- Rating reuse without permission: AllSides COO/CFO Alice Griesemer Sheehan says Ground News used AllSides ratings without "formal permission" and without compensation. [VERIFIED as reported by: https://www.cjr.org/analysis/the-business-of-balance-ground-news.php]
- Paid vs unpaid: Ad Fontes CEO Vanessa Otero says Ground News pays for its ratings. Ground News: "We don't disclose ongoing business relationships and arrangements." [VERIFIED: same CJR URL]
- MBFC: founder Dave Van Zandt says MBFC "does not have an agreement with Ground News." [VERIFIED: same CJR URL]
- MBFC's own review of Ground News: Least Biased (0.0) and Mostly Factual (2.2), but it notes Ground News does "not factor in the credibility of the source" when linking stories (examples PJ Media, Sputnik, Newswars). Ground News relies on MBFC as one of its raters, and MBFC's page does not address this conflict. [VERIFIED: https://mediabiasfactcheck.com/ground-news/]
- "Automated aggregation" and paywalled factuality: CJR author Martina di Licosa. [SECONDARY: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ground_News citing CJR 2025]
- "False equivalency" in the rating method: cited on Wikipedia to a single blog post by Eric Rosenfield. Weak source; treat as [UNVERIFIED]. [SECONDARY: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ground_News]
- Blindspot effect: Curtis Bram (2024) found 3 of 4 stories did not change views; one shifted Republican subscribers on refugee policy. A 2024 study found Blindspots "may be a promising method to mitigate polarization." Mixed evidence. [SECONDARY: CJR and Wikipedia URLs above]
- Sponsorship: Axios (2025), citing Gospel Stats, reported Ground News was the most common YouTube sponsor in its sample. [SECONDARY: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ground_News]
- Ground News is owned by Snapwise Inc. (Ontario, Canada), funded by subscriptions. [VERIFIED: https://mediabiasfactcheck.com/ground-news/]

## 4. Free data access (for building our own ratings)

- No free, redistributable bulk dataset from any of the three raters was confirmed.
- MBFC: data is commercial or RapidAPI-limited. Community scrapes exist (for example mikeizbicki/mediabiasfactcheck on GitHub, a mirror-to-CSV script; a gist with a "fully scrubbed CSV" from nsfyn55). Their license and current accuracy are unknown. A search summary advised checking MBFC's terms before reuse, because ratings may be copyrighted; MBFC's own statement on this was not found (ToS not retrieved). [UNVERIFIED]
- Ad Fontes: paid CSV from weekly/monthly tiers. Academic use is listed as a use case, but no free tier was found. [SECONDARY: https://adfontesmedia.com/?p=91858]
- AllSides: no official bulk download found. GitHub copy favstats/allsider (GPL-3.0 for code, data license unclear). [SECONDARY]
- Alternative free dataset (not one of the three): idiap/news-media-reliability (domain reliability labels, on GitHub and Hugging Face). Its license and coverage were not checked. [SECONDARY: https://awesome.ecosyste.ms/projects/github.com%2Fidiap%2Fnews-media-reliability]
- Recommendation for V: do not ingest rater data without written permission. Build our own rating for Finnish outlets (see section 5) or license MBFC/Ad Fontes data.
