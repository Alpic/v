# Ground News audit: insights, newsletters, extension, tiers, reviews

Scope: Ground News personal/insight features and business. Meeting 1, researcher topic.
Method: WebSearch + WebFetch of Ground News pages and third-party sources. Every claim has a URL or is marked [UNVERIFIED]. Prices and tier contents change often; re-check at checkout before any decision. Raw pages were not saved; WebFetch returned extracted answers only.

## 1. Blindspot feed and Blindspot Report

- **Blindspot feed:** highlights "news topics being covered by only one side of the political spectrum," to help readers "step outside of their personal news bubble." Source: https://help.ground.news/en/articles/485057
- **Blindspot Report (weekly newsletter):** "Each week, the Ground News Blindspot Report looks at the platform's data to identify the news events from the past week that received the most lopsided coverage." Method per the page: uses the day's top 100 stories and evaluates "the mean and standard deviation of the aggregate bias ratings"; stories with the highest mean or highest standard deviation are chosen. No price is stated on the page. Source: https://about.ground.news/blindspot-subscribe
- Selection is algorithm plus editors; a blog says Blindspot "can only highlight what Ground News' algorithms and editors have identified" [UNVERIFIED, low-quality blog, https://brainmindsociety.com/blog/ground-news-understanding-the-blindspot].
- Free vs paid Blindspot: the StationX review says free users get 6 Blindspot stories/day [UNVERIFIED, affiliate review, see section 9]. Ground's own pages say Premium/Vantage get "unlimited access to the Blindspot feed" (section 4).

## 2. My News Bias dashboard

- Ground FAQ: it "exposes the user's own reading behaviors in the Beta feature 'My News Bias'." The FAQ does not say what metrics it measures. Source: https://about.ground.news/frequently-asked-questions
- Help article (search snippet, third-party-cited): "Blindspot story suggestions, what controversial issues are happening in the world today, the overall ownership data and factuality score of the news you've been reading." [UNVERIFIED as to exact wording]
- Search summary says the dashboard shows "the number of articles you read, the bias in the reporting, and historical insights" [UNVERIFIED].
- Charts: I could not confirm any chart types, axes or screenshots from an official source. Ground's subscribe page lists "My News Bias dashboard" and "Reading habit analytics" as features (https://ground.news/subscribe) [chart types UNVERIFIED].
- Access: Vantage help article says "The more you read, the more insights you'll find in My News Bias" and that Vantage unlocks it. Premium's described features do not mention it (https://help.ground.news/en/articles/648513). A search snippet says base plan has "Limited My News Bias Access" and Premium "Unlimited My News Bias analysis" [UNVERIFIED, from a pricing page snippet; conflicts with help article].
- Algorithm claim conflict: FAQ says My Feed is "algorithmically curated based on stated and observed reading behaviors"; a second Ground page reportedly says "We don't use algorithms to shape what you see" [UNVERIFIED, source not fetched]. Flag for copy: do not repeat either claim as fact.

## 3. Bias ratings (underlying data for the dashboard, Blindspot and Report)

- Bias ratings are "averaged from the published ratings by three non-profit news publication monitoring organizations": AllSides, Ad Fontes Media, Media Bias Fact Check. "The Ground team takes no part in evaluating a Bias Rating." Source: https://about.ground.news/frequently-asked-questions
- Help article: ratings for "thousands of sources", "three independent organizations", team aims "to keep our team's subjective opinions out of the product", dispute process exists. Source: https://help.ground.news/en/articles/485057
- Factuality ratings: Vantage includes "factuality and bias rating data" (help 648513). A search summary says factuality is paywalled while bias is free [UNVERIFIED].
- Limitation (independent reviewer, affiliate, see section 9): raters skew US-centric; factuality is publisher-level, not article-level [UNVERIFIED, StationX].

## 6. Browser extension

- Store page: "Whenever you come across a news article online, the Ground News browser extension shows you what other sources are reporting on that story." Shows source comparison on news stories. Store links: Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Brave, Opera. Source: https://ground.news/extension
- On non-news sites: the page mentions Twitter, Facebook and Reddit as contexts, but does not say what is displayed there [UNVERIFIED behavior].
- Permissions and login requirements: not stated on the page.
- Firefox add-on "Ground News Bias Checker" (search results; not fetched):
  - Mixed reviews. Mozilla reviewer: extension "sends every url you visit back to ground news" (reviewer claim, not verified) [UNVERIFIED].
  - Complaints: broad permissions, high CPU and page-rendering issues on unrelated sites, daily re-login friction (2026 review), login failure despite browser auth [UNVERIFIED].
  - Ratings: firefox-stats shows all-time 3.25 (28 ratings), recent 2.30 [UNVERIFIED, third-party aggregator]. Sources: https://addons.mozilla.org/en-GB/firefox/addon/ground-news-bias-checker/reviews and https://firefox-stats.com/d/ground-news-bias-checker
- Audit implication: extension is the weakest-rated surface. Permission scope and URL-sharing claims are the highest-risk items to check against the manifest before we build anything similar.

## 8. Reviews: praise and complaints

Ratings (primary-source where possible):
- **App Store (US):** 4.7 from 49K ratings (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ground-news/id1324203419, fetched). A search summary's "3.8 from 42 ratings" conflicts and is likely stale or wrong [UNVERIFIED].
- **Google Play:** no official figure retrieved. Aggregator: 4.64 (31,746) for v4.30.0, dated 2026-08-06 [UNVERIFIED, marlvel/apptail-type aggregator]. Another summary says 4.7 (23.4K) [UNVERIFIED].
- **Trustpilot:** TrustScore 4.2 from 232 reviews (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/ground.news, fetched). Company replies to negative reviews. Sample of about 21 visible reviews: 18 five-star, 3 one-star.

Most praised (recurring across sources):
1. Balanced coverage and seeing how the political spectrum covers a story: App Store reviews; Trustpilot sample (about 8 of 21 visible reviews).
2. Bias transparency and comparison tools: Common Sense Media and search summaries [UNVERIFIED for Common Sense].
3. Fast customer service, easy refunds (Trustpilot sample, about 10 of 21).
4. Short, clear summaries; "tools to evaluate the news for myself" (Trustpilot quote, Aimee F).

Most complained about (recurring):
1. Cancellation and billing: "They make it impossible to cancel subscription" (Trustpilot, Gregory Wright); "charged after cancelled" (Trustpilot, Pat Pascoli). Also trial renewal at full price [UNVERIFIED roundup].
2. Paywalls and feature gating: free users frustrated; paywall for ownership data and full dashboard (StationX, affiliate; aggregator roundups) [UNVERIFIED].
3. Extension: permissions, privacy, CPU/performance, login friction (Firefox reviews) [UNVERIFIED, see section 6].
4. App stability and speed: crashes, slow performance (aggregator roundups) [UNVERIFIED].
5. Bias-rating quality: syndicated wire reprints rated per outlet, not per article (Trustpilot, one reviewer); US-centric raters (StationX) [UNVERIFIED for StationX].
6. Political bias perception of the product itself (one Play review on a third-party site) [UNVERIFIED].

Reddit: not reachable. The search tool refused reddit.com as a domain (400 error). Reddit-titled search results were generic blog posts with no real threads, so no Reddit findings are included. Gap: needs a manual pass on r/news and r/MediaBias or a working Reddit source.
